How it works

From EEG to lesson in five stages.

The headset streams raw EEG. The pipeline turns it into an attention span four times a second, and a rule-based policy combines that span with your answers to decide how the next lesson is written.

Stage 1

Headset

Prototype hardware: OpenBCI Ganglion, 4 channels over Bluetooth LE. Default montage F3, F4, O1, O2.

µV @ 200 Hz
Stage 2

Band powers

BrainFlow computes relative δ θ α β γ power over a sliding 5–20 s window, with detrend, notch and band-pass.

x ∈ ℝ⁵, every 250 ms
Stage 3

Attention span

The classifier's logit, smoothed with a 4 s time constant and scaled between your calibrated Relaxed and Focused states.

span ∈ [0, 1] @ 4 Hz
Stage 4

Policy

A 3 × 2 outcome grid maps comprehension and attention to the next move, plus at most two style changes per lesson.

booster · format · level
Stage 5

Lesson renderer

An LLM writes the lesson in the chosen style. Output is schema-validated and style constraints are checked in code.

Technology

From a binary readout to a continuous attention span.

The stock classifier saturates near p = 1.0, so its focus light is almost always on. We recovered the model's weights, undid its sigmoid and calibrated the result to each learner.

Why the stock readout is binary

At the operating point, a full unit change in the decision value moves p only in the third decimal. NeuroAdapt reads the horizontal axis instead.

Recovered classifier weights

Recovered by finite differences, verified to three decimals on random band vectors. The large gamma weight means jaw and forehead tension can inflate the score.

The span, in three steps

s = ln(p / (1 − p)) = w · xLinearize: undo the sigmoid to get the logit.
s̃ ← s̃ + (1 − e−Δt/τ)(s − s̃), τ = 4 sSmooth with a time-aware exponential moving average.
span = (s̃ − L) / (H − L)Scale between your captured Relaxed (L) and Focused (H) means.

Calibrated per learner

Capture a minute relaxed and a minute focused. Their means become 0 and 1 on your scale. The span is never an absolute percentage and never compared across people.

Teaching policy

The LLM writes content. Code makes every decision. Comprehension comes from rubric marks (pass ≥ 0.75, partial 0.40–0.75, miss < 0.40); attention is high when the lesson's mean span ≥ 0.50.

High attentionLow attention
PassAdvance. No booster.Suggest a short refresher in a new format.
PartialShort booster and a recheck.Short booster in a new format and a recheck.
MissLong booster, different representation, one level simpler.Long booster in a new format and a recheck.

At most two style changes per lesson, so any later change in attention stays attributable. No headset: the policy falls back to comprehension alone.
